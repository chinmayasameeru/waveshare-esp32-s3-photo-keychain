Waveshare Photo Display

Direct browser flashing for the ESP32-S3-LCD-2 No-Touch board.

ESP32-S3R8 16 MB Flash 8 MB PSRAM 240×320 ST7789T3
Use desktop Chrome or Edge for Web Serial. This installer must be opened over HTTPS.
After flashing
  1. Let the board reboot.
  2. On your iPhone, join the PhotoDisplay-xxxxxx Wi-Fi network.
  3. Password: photo1234
  4. Open http://192.168.4.1 in Safari.
  5. Choose a JPEG and press Show photo.

The firmware stores the JPEG in FFat and displays it on the LCD. Wi-Fi times out after 10 minutes without browser activity.
