After installation

The USB installer is only the setup gate. After reboot, the board can enter its local photo workflow from the BOOT button.

1
Connect over USBUse a known-good data cable and select the ESP32-S3 port when prompted.
2
Press BOOTThe keychain starts its temporary local Wi‑Fi access point and shows connection details on the LCD.
3
Open the local pageJoin the keychain's Wi‑Fi network from your phone, then use the local address shown on the display.
4
Choose a photoThe transfer workflow is intended to stay local to the keychain and phone.

Before you flash

Use the No-Touch board

This experiment targets the Waveshare ESP32-S3 2-inch No-Touch display and ST7789T3 wiring. The Touch-LCD-2 variant uses different hardware.

What is experimental?

The iPhone transfer, power behavior, and keychain experience are still under development. Treat this as an experiment rather than the stable release.
